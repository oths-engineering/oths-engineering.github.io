# Tompkins Engineering Design Club

A student-led engineering design club turning ambitious ideas into real-world builds through collaboration, curiosity, and hands-on making.

## about

Tompkins Engineering Design Club is a community for students who want to design, build, and understand how things work.

From the first sketch to the final test, we bring together curious minds across engineering disciplines. Learn 3D design, work with real tools, and find a team that makes big ideas feel possible.
